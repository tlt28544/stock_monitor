Data Collection (Adaptive Tavily)
Each ticker gets 3 targeted searches instead of exhaustive news scraping. Coverage optimized for relevance over completeness.
Search Queries
  • Query 1: {company} earnings revenue financial results quarterly (max 10)
  • Query 2: {company} product launch competition market share (max 10)
  • Query 3: {company} regulatory industry sector trends (max 10)
Output
  • ~30 articles per ticker (vs 4000-5000 with EODHD)
  • Each article: title, URL, published_date, summary
  • Deduplication: O(n) URL-based (vs O(n²) content similarity)

3
Narrative Synthesis (NarrativeResearch Provider)
Transform classified events into 3-5 deep narrative blocks with connected storytelling, data-backed analysis, and institutional investor tone.
LLM Prompt Instructions
  • Write 3-5 thematic narrative blocks (not bullet lists)
  • Each block: 200-600 words of flowing prose
  • Include specific numbers, dates, context, and implications
  • Cite event IDs inline for traceability
  • Add "Key Metrics" summary box after each block
Output Structure (NarrativeBlock)
  • title: Narrative block headline
  • body: 200-600 word connected story
  • key_metrics: Bullet-list summary of key data points
  • event_ids: Cited event references
Example Block: Title: Strong Earnings Acceleration Driven by Revenue Growth and Margin Expansion Body: Amazon's most recent earnings reports reveal an exceptionally robust performance both in top-line growth and profitability margins over the past year. Third quarter 2025 results showed revenue of $180.2 billion, a 13% increase from the prior year quarter, with net income climbing 38% to $21.2 billion and profit margin expanding to 12% from 9.6%. More strikingly, the second quarter of 2026 displayed a dramatic acceleration with revenue reaching $200.6 billion, up 20% year-over-year... Key Metrics: • Q3 2025 Revenue: $180.2B (↑13%) • Q3 2025 Net Income: $21.2B (↑38%) • Q2 2026 Revenue: $200.6B (↑20%) • Q2 2026 Profit Margin: 31% (from 11%)

4
Market Data Enrichment (EODHD API)
Fetch real-time market context: price, market cap, P/E ratio, and valuation flags from high-materiality events.
Data Sources
  • EODHD real-time quote API: GET /api/real-time/{TICKER}.US
  • Returns: close price, market cap, P/E ratio, timestamp
  • Graceful degradation: missing data shows as "N/A"
Valuation Table
  • Currently empty (no analyst reference model)
  • Placeholder for future SOTP breakdown
  • Flags high-materiality events for review
↓
5
PDF Report Generation (narrative_reports.py)
Render narrative blocks into a professional PDF with market context header, flowing prose, key metrics boxes, source index, and valuation table.
Report Structure
  • Header: Ticker | Price | Market Cap | P/E | Report Date
  • Narrative Blocks: 3-5 thematic sections (200-600 words each)
  • Key Metrics Boxes: Bullet summary after each block
  • Sources & Event Index: Hyperlinked citations
  • Valuation Review: Assumptions to revisit
  • Valuation Table: SOTP framework (placeholder)
Validation
  • PDF must be 2-4 pages
  • Ticker symbol present
  • "Sources" and "Valuation" sections exist
  • All event source URLs embedded as hyperlinks
  • SHA256 hash integrity check
